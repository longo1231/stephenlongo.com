date: 2026-06-10

This is the first post in a new, deliberately informal corner of the site. Less polished than an essay, more permanent than a tweet.

The workflow is simple: write a page in the Musings database in Notion, check **Published**, and it shows up here on the next sync. Drafts stay drafts until the box is checked.

That's it. Lower the bar, write more.
